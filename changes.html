What's new

Play the gameGame guideAll scoresWhat's new

0.9.4 28 October 2026

  • The game has a new address: steppecrawl.online. Your saved game and local scores stay in the browser you played in; the shared scoreboard came along.

0.9.3 9 August 2026

  • Dust devil traps no longer carry you through closed doors.
  • The Herald of Morkhan no longer forgets you if you step back through the cleft.

0.9 14 June 2025

  • Shared scoreboard on the start page, with the ten best games and the ten most recent deaths. You can turn it off on the title screen.
  • The game guide is now a page of its own (guide.html) as well as the ? key in the game.

0.8.2 2 February 2025

  • Resting stops when a creature comes into view, as it always should have.

0.8 17 November 2024

  • Plays on a phone: arrow pad and buttons under the map, tap to travel, tap twice to fire.
  • The game saves itself in your browser as you play. Close the tab and carry on later from the title screen.

0.7 23 March 2024

  • Two new regions: the Old Kurgans and the Pass of the Black Blizzard, each with a clan tamga at the far end.
  • The cleft under Manas Peak now opens for any three of the four tamgas.
  • Once you take the Ene Malka, the valleys come after you on the way home.

0.6.1 26 November 2023

  • The whole game is loaded at the start and then needs no connection at all, so a lost signal no longer ends a run.

0.6 19 August 2023

  • Regions off the road: the Smugglers' Tunnels, the Arslanbob Woods and the Moiynkum Sands, with their keepers.
  • Clan tamgas. The sealed cleft now needs them.

0.5 8 January 2023

  • Tiles! The game now uses the public-domain Dungeon Crawl 32x32 tiles, with our own for Iradi things. The ASCII version stays, and the two share one saved game.

0.4 21 May 2022

  • Patrons. Pray at an altar or a mazar, and keep your patron happy.
  • The White Yurts, where every patron has an altar.

0.3 11 December 2021

  • Peoples and callings: choose who you are and what you do before you ride out. Try a Tash-Kishi Kashysh if you want to heal.
  • Skills and experience, with aptitudes by people.

0.2 5 June 2021

  • Ten valleys from Kara-Oi to Manas Peak, each a day farther from home.
  • The Hidden Valley of Janyl, the Herald of Morkhan and the Ene Malka. Bring it home to win.

0.1 7 March 2021

  • First playable version. One Baatyr, five valleys, ASCII only, and death is permanent.
